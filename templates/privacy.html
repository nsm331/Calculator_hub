Privacy Policy

We respect your absolute privacy. Learn how data is handled on CalculatorHub.

Client-Side Local Computation

Calculator inputs (such as income, debt balances, height, or weight) are processed directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit or store your calculation inputs on our servers.

Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense and third-party advertising cookies to display contextual advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Server Logs

Standard web server logs collect non-identifiable diagnostic metrics (e.g. IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL) solely to maintain security and server performance.
