💡 Why Sleep Cycles Matter

Waking up midway through an ultradian cycle (during Stage N3 Slow-Wave Sleep) forces the brain to abruptly reactivate while inundated with delta waves, resulting in severe sleep inertia (brain fog, impaired motor reaction times, and fatigue). Waking at the end of a 90-minute cycle ensures your brain transitions smoothly through light sleep (Stage N1/N2) for effortless awakening.
